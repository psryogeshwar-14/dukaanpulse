# DukaanPulse: Merchant Discovery & Validation Framework
**Target Audience:** Independent Café Owners, Quick-Service Food Outlets, Local Bakeries  
**Author:** Product & QA Lead  
**Date:** November 21, 2026

---

## 1. Ethical Research Protocol

- **Zero Fabrication**: All responses recorded in this document must come from live, in-person, or video interviews with real café proprietors.
- **No Testimonial Manufacturing**: Fake reviews, simulated owner quotes, and hypothetical revenue claims are strictly prohibited.
- **Hypothesis Status**: DukaanPulse treats the value proposition as an unvalidated problem hypothesis to be empirically tested against merchants, not a pre-proven conclusion.

---

## 2. Core Merchant Interview Questions

The following six questions must be asked to at least 3 local café managers or owners (e.g., in HSR Layout, Koramangala, or Indiranagar, Bengaluru):

1. **"What was the last promotional combo or new menu offer you tried?"**
   - *Objective*: Identify actual recent experiment behavior (e.g., coffee combo, evening snack, weekday discount).
2. **"How did you decide on its selling price?"**
   - *Objective*: Understand pricing methodology (e.g., guessing, copying nearby competitors, rough markup).
3. **"Did you calculate your exact variable cost per order (ingredients + packaging) beforehand?"**
   - *Objective*: Test whether merchants track unit contribution or mix fixed overheads into pricing.
4. **"What local market information did you check before launching?"**
   - *Objective*: Determine if they checked Swiggy/Zomato listings, Google Maps reviews, walked around the block, or launched blind.
5. **"How did you decide whether the offer actually worked or failed?"**
   - *Objective*: Reveal measurement criteria (e.g., gut feeling, cash drawer total, POS report, inventory waste).
6. **"What was the most difficult or frustrating part of that entire process?"**
   - *Objective*: Surface pain points around time constraints, fear of wasting food/budget, and lack of clear guidance.
